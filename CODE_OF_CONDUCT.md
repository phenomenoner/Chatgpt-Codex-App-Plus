# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss the work rather than the person; welcome good-faith questions; protect private information; and do not use this project to facilitate unauthorized access, harassment, deception, or harm.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, unsafe, off-topic, or privacy-violating contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct or privacy concerns privately through the repository's security or maintainer contact channels when a public issue would expose someone to risk.
